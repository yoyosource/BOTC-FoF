# Chemist
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night, eye the cauldron; either choose 1) 2 potions to be traded, or 2) a good potion another player holds; you receive it, theirs break.

*Life is just chemicals. A drop here, a drip there, everything's changed.*

The Chemist exerts control over Potions in the Cauldron moving them around in the most favourable way for their team.

## How to run

Each night, wake the Chemist.
Start showing them the Cauldron.
Have them signal 1 or 2.

If they signaled 1, have them choose 2 potions held by different living players.
Exchange the places of those 2 potions.

If they signaled 2, have them choose 1 good potion held by a living player (not themselves).
Remove it from that player, by breaking it, and add it onto the Chemist, by them receiving it.

Put the Chemist to sleep.

## Examples

Ida the Chemist wakes up and is shown the Cauldron, then signals 2.
She points at Bob's Potion of Empathy, a good potion.
Bob's Potion of Empathy breaks and Ida receives a Potion of Empathy.

Milo the Chemist wakes up and signals 1.
He points at Carla's Potion of Joy and Deng's Potion of Apathy, and the Storyteller swaps the two potions' places.
Carla now holds the Potion of Apathy and Deng now holds the Potion of Joy.

Priya the Chemist wakes up and signals 2, but the only potions other players hold are a Potion of Terror and a Potion of Panic.
Neither is a good potion, so there is nothing for option 2 to take.

## Tips & Tricks

- Each night you either swap two players' potions or steal a good potion for yourself and break the original, so mix things up in whatever way most favours your team.
- Stealing a good potion is a clean two-for-one: the town loses the benefit and you gain it, all while their copy breaks.
- Swapping lets you reposition the board - move an evil potion onto a townsfolk, or pull a helpful potion away from someone who would use it well.
- Holding a good potion you have stolen also helps you look town-aligned when you bluff.
- Eyeing the Cauldron each night gives you a full read of where every potion sits and which are evil.
- Option 2 only works on good potions, so when none are out there, fall back on a swap.

## Fighting the Chemist

- The Chemist reshuffles potions every night, so a good potion vanishing or a potion appearing on a new player can be its work - watch the Cauldron for unexplained moves.
- A helpful potion that goes missing and turns up on another player points to a Chemist steal, so a player suddenly holding someone else's good potion is a suspect.
- Because it eyes the Cauldron each night, it knows where your evil potions sit, so do not assume those locations are secret from the evil team.
- Trace a chain of odd potion movements back to who benefits, as the Chemist tends to leave the board arranged in evil's favour.
- Executing the Chemist stops the nightly potion manipulation, which is worth doing once its shuffling gives it away.
