# Aberration
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When a Potion of Panic is unveiled, a player is poisoned until dawn. Each night, eye the cauldron and choose a player to receive a Potion of Panic.

*There are horrors beyond life's edge that we do not suspect, and once in a while man's evil prying calls them just within our range.*

What a nice little fella.
The Aberration adds misinformation and mechanical disruption to the evil team, and interacts uniquely with the Megalomaniac and the Tramp.

## How to run

During the day if a Potion of Panic is unveiled (shown face up in the Cauldron) you decide which player is poisoned until dawn.
Put the **POISONED** reminder on the chosen player.

Each night, wake the Aberration.
Start showing them the Cauldron.
They point at any living player.
Give the chosen player a Potion of Panic
Put the Aberration to sleep.

## Examples

Nadia the Aberration wakes up and is shown the Cauldron.
She points at Leo, and the Storyteller puts a Potion of Panic on Leo.

During the day, Quill asks the Storyteller to unveil his potions and a Potion of Panic is shown face up in the Cauldron.
Because a Potion of Panic was just unveiled, the Storyteller may poison a player until dawn.
The Storyteller puts the **POISONED** reminder token on Rhea - it does not have to be the player who unveiled it - and Rhea's ability malfunctions until dawn.

Later that same day, Wren also unveils a Potion of Panic.
As this is a new unveiling, the Storyteller may again choose a player to poison until dawn.

## Tips & Tricks

- Each night you place a Potion of Panic on a player, and while it stays veiled it looks just like a good potion, so you quietly seed the board with disguised evil tokens.
- Whenever a Potion of Panic is unveiled during the day a player is poisoned until dawn, which can shut off a townsfolk's ability for the coming night.
- Since you create the Panics yourself, you control the supply that can later be unveiled to trigger that poison.
- Call up a little horror where it hurts most: you choose who receives each Panic, so place it on a player you suspect will unveil their potions the most the next day.
- Put a Potion of Panic on yourself from time to time, since a veiled Panic looks like a good potion and holding one helps you blend in and avoids the tell of never being a recipient.
- Eye the Cauldron each night to read the current potion state and decide where a fresh Panic does the most damage.
- You pair naturally with a Tramp, who also floods the board with Panic, and with a Megalomaniac demon, whose extra action a broken veiled Panic can trigger.

## Fighting the Aberration

- Unveiling potions is dangerous while an Aberration is in play, since revealing a Potion of Panic causes a player to be poisoned until dawn.
- A veiled Potion of Panic looks exactly like a good potion, so a potion you were about to trust and flip could be an Aberration's trap.
- Track who keeps being handed potions that turn out to be Panic, as the Aberration is seeding them each night, though a good Tramp may be adding some too.
- If a strong information role suddenly gives bad results the night after a Panic was unveiled, suspect an Aberration poison rather than the role being evil.
- Executing the Aberration stops both the nightly Panic supply and the unveil-poison, so it is a high-value execution once you have found it.
