# Aberration
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When a Potion of Panic is revealed, a player is poisoned until dawn. Each night, eye the cauldron and choose a player to receive a Potion of Panic.

*There are horrors beyond life's edge that we do not suspect, and once in a while man's evil prying calls them just within our range.*
