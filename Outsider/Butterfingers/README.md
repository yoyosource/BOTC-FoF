# Butterfingers
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When you are executed, all Townsfolk players are drunk until dusk.

*It took a special kind of man to cut his sword hand with his own sword.*

Butterfingers is an Outsider designed to cause hesitation in town for its execution, acting both as a potential hazard and a strong bluff.

## How to run

If the Butterfingers is executed put the **OOPS** reminder token on the Butterfingers.
Any townsfolk ability will not work until dusk and remove the **OOPS** reminder token.
