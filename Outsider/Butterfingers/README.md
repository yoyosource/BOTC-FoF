# Butterfingers
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When you are executed, all Townsfolk players are drunk until dusk.

*It took a special kind of man to cut his sword hand with his own sword.*

Butterfingers is an Outsider designed to cause hesitation in town for its execution, acting both as a potential hazard and a strong bluff.

## How to run

If the Butterfingers is executed put the **OOPS** reminder token on the Butterfingers.
Any townsfolk ability will not work until dusk and remove the **OOPS** reminder token.

## Tips & Tricks

- Executing you turns every Townsfolk drunk until dusk, so the town disrupts its own information by voting you out, making you a costly execution.
- Let the town know you are the Butterfingers so they think twice before spending a day drunking themselves on you.
- Your ability only fires on execution, so a night kill wastes it - staying an obvious execution risk can steer the demon into killing you instead and sparing a real townsfolk.
- On a knife-edge day, letting yourself be executed can blunt the town's next round of information, so choose when you are willing to go to the block.

## Bluffing as the Butterfingers

- Claiming it discourages the town from executing you, since they fear drunking themselves, giving an evil player a cheap shield.
- There is nothing to prove while you live, so the claim cannot be checked from one day to the next.
- The catch is that being executed exposes you, since the Townsfolk will not actually go drunk and the town learns your claim was false.
- Because of that, try to have your own demon kill you at night instead of ever being executed, so you die looking like a good player the demon removed and your bluff is never put to the test.
- Save the claim for deterring a vote rather than for surviving an actual execution.

## Fighting the Butterfingers

The Clofou can secretly turn an Outsider evil, so a Butterfingers you thought was good may now be working for evil. This section covers how the good team can play against a hidden Butterfingers.

- A turned Butterfingers keeps its ability, so executing it still drunks every Townsfolk until dusk - its death by vote removes an evil player but blinds your own information at the same time.
- Watch its eagerness: a Butterfingers pushing the town to execute it on a pivotal day may have flipped and be baiting you into that drunkenness.
- If you do execute a suspected turned Butterfingers, pick a day you can afford to lose your Townsfolk information rather than one where the coming night is critical.
- A Butterfingers that suddenly acts oddly or against the town can help you locate the Clofou, since it must have neighboured or traded with the demon to be turned.
- Do not treat a Butterfingers claim as automatically harmless; once turned, that same costly-execution shield now protects the evil team.
