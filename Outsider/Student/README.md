# Student
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> You may only vote or nominate if you have a trade active.

*Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire.*

The Student is an obstructive Outsider, as it cannot be trusted to be able to vote or nominate at its own leisure.
It also works as a good bluff for evil players that wishes to utilize the trade system.

## How to run
The Student may only nominate a person if they have a trade active.
During the nomination, the Student may only have their hand raised to vote if they have a trade active.

To have a trade active means that you have asked to trade with a player and they have accepted.
If no player accepts a trade with the Student, they are unable to vote or nominate that day.

## Examples
Tobi the Student asked Sandra to trade and she agreed.
During the nomination Tobi is allowed to vote on Christopher's nomination.
After that Tobi nominates Lisa.

## Tips & Tricks

- You can only vote or nominate on a day you have a trade active, so do your homework early and line up a trade partner before nominations if you want a say.
- A trade is active once another player agrees to it, so ask around in good time rather than scrambling once a key nomination is on the table.
- Prioritise having a trade on days a big execution is likely, since a day with no trade leaves you sitting on your hands.
- Trading to secure your vote also moves potions between you and your partner, so pick a partner whose potions you are happy to swap.

## Bluffing as the Student

- The Student is a natural bluff for an evil player who wants to trade a lot, since constant trading looks like you are simply trying to keep your vote.
- It gives you cover to sit out votes you would rather not cast, by claiming you had no trade active that day.
- Your trades let you shuffle potions around your team while explaining it away as securing your vote.
- There is no night action to fake, so the only thing to role-play is the daytime voting restriction.
- Occasionally make a show of scrambling for a trade before a vote to sell the role.

## Fighting the Student

The Clofou can secretly turn an Outsider evil, so a Student you thought was good may now be working for evil. This section covers how the good team can play against a hidden Student.

- A turned Student still needs a trade active to vote or nominate, so on important votes it must trade first - watch who it partners with and what potions move as a result.
- A Student that suddenly acts oddly or against the town can point you toward the Clofou, since it must have neighboured or traded with the demon to be turned.
- Good players should refuse to trade with a Student suspected of having turned evil; if it still finds a trade, whoever traded with it becomes a suspect too.
- Remember its trades can quietly move evil potions onto the town, so treat a turned Student's eager trading as more than just a bid to vote.
