# Student
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> You may only vote or nominate if you have a trade active.

*Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire.*

The Student is an obstructive Outsider, as it cannot be trusted to be able to vote or nominate at its own leisure.
It also works as a good bluff for evil players that wishes to utilize the trade system.

## How to run
The Student may only nominate a person if they have a trade active.
During the nomination, the Student may only have their hand raised to vote if they have a trade active.

To have a trade active means that you have asked to trade with a player and they have accepted.
If no player accepts a trade with the Student, they are unable to vote or nominate that day.

## Examples

Tobi the Student asked Sandra to trade and she agreed.
During the nomination Tobi is allowed to vote on Christopher's nomination.
After that Tobi nominates Lisa.
