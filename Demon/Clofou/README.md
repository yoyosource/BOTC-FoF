# Clofou
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night*, choose a player; they die. The first time you neighbour or trade with a living Outsider, they become evil. [+1 Outsider]

*The saints were his friends, and blessed him; the monsters were his friends, and guarded him.*

The Clofou is a Demon that adds uncertainty about the Outsider count and causes paranoia over the alignment of Outsiders in the game.

## How to run

While setting up the game, before putting character tokens in the bag, add one extra Outsider character token and remove one Townsfolk character token.

On each execution of a living player if the Clofou now has a living neighbour that is an Outsider and there is no evil Outsider in play rotate the Outsiders character token upside-down for evil.
The following night wake the Outsider give them a thumbs-down (indicating their new alignment), then put them back to sleep.

When the Clofou trades with an Outsider if there is no evil Outsider in play rotate the Outsiders character token upside-down for evil.
Wake the Outsider give them a thumbs-down (indicating their new alignment), then put them back to sleep.

Each night except the first, wake the Clofou.
They point at any player.
Put the Clofou to sleep.
That player dies - mark them with the **DEAD** reminder.
If at this Point the Clofou has a living neighbour that is an Outsider and there is no evil Outsider in play rotate the Outsiders character token upside-down for evil.
Then wake the Outsider give them a thumbs-down (indicating their new alignment), then put them back to sleep.

## Examples

Marla the Clofou wakes up on the 2nd night and chooses Tom, her neighbour, to die.
With Tom dead, Marla's nearest living neighbour is now Nora the Sommelier, an Outsider, and no Outsider is evil yet.
The Storyteller rotates Nora's character token upside-down for evil right away and wakes Nora and gives her a thumbs-down to show her new alignment.

Bruno the Clofou publicly trades potions with Ella the Student, an Outsider.
Since no Outsider is evil yet, the Storyteller rotates Ella's character token upside-down for evil and wakes her and gives her a thumbs-down.
Ella is now an evil player.

Cormac the Clofou already turned an Outsider evil earlier this game.
Tonight Cormac chooses a player to die and now neighbours Finn the Tramp, another living Outsider.
Because there is already an evil Outsider in play, nothing happens to Finn - he stays good.

## Tips & Tricks

- You begin with an extra Outsider added at setup, so the town cannot trust its Outsider count from the very first day.
- The first time you neighbour or trade with a living Outsider, they turn evil and join you, so getting close to an Outsider hands you a free extra teammate.
- Make a monster of a friend: engineer that meeting by killing the players between you and an Outsider to become their neighbour, or simply trade with one.
- The conversion only happens once, and only while no Outsider is evil yet, so pick the Outsider whose ability or seat helps evil the most.
- A turned Outsider keeps its ability but now wins with you and votes with evil, so it can act as a trusted-looking mouthpiece for your team.

## Fighting the Clofou

- The Clofou adds an extra Outsider and can turn one evil, so treat both the Outsider count and every Outsider's alignment as uncertain.
- An Outsider that suddenly acts oddly or against the town may have been turned, which also points you toward where the Clofou has been sitting or trading.
- Once one Outsider has turned evil, no others can, so a single flipped Outsider means the rest are safe from this conversion.
- If you are an Outsider, be careful who you trade with and sit beside, since neighbouring or trading with the Clofou is exactly what turns you evil.
- Otherwise hunt it like any demon: follow the night kills and work back to who is bluffing a good role.
