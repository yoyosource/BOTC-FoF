# Potion of Empathy
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night you learn what good and evil potions your living neighbors are holding.

*Now I know for certain; you feel for me as I feel for you.*

A source of information on the types of potions held by some of the other players, without them having to be unveiled.

## How to run

Each night wake any player holding a Potion of Empathy after one another.
Show them all potions that their living neighbors are holding.
They do not learn which neighbor holds which, and they do not learn multiples of types held by both.

## Examples

Brian is holding a Potion of Empathy, so at night the Storyteller wakes them and shows them the Potion of Conviction and the Potion of Apathy.
This is due to their right-side neighbor Victor holding a Potion of Conviction and their left-side neighbor Carla holding a Potion of Apathy.

Roger learns Potion of Curiosity with their Potion of Empathy.
Both their neighbors are holding a Potion of Curiosity each, but they are still only shown the Potion of Curiosity once.

Clement's Potion of Empathy reveals no information to them at night, even though one of their living neighbors is holding a Potion.
This is because that potion is a Potion of Panic, which is neither Good nor Evil, and therefor is not revealed by the Potion of Empathy.
