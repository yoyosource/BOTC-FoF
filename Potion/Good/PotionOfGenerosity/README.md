# Potion of Generosity
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When this potion is traded, an evil potion part of that trade breaks. If an evil potion breaks this way, this potion breaks.

*I give that you may give.*

The Potion of Generosity can be used to remove evil Potions from world building and help the players remove potentially bad potions.

## How to run
When a Potion of Generosity it traded and either party is holding an evil potion the storyteller breaks one of the evil potions and the Potion of Generosity before the trade happens.

## Examples
Roe is holding a Potion of Generosity and a Potion of Apathy.
Roe accepted to trade with Marcus holding a Potion of Empathy.
Tonight the before the trade happens both the Potion of Generosity and Potion of Apathy break.
Afterward Roe receives the Potion of Empathy from Marcus and Marcus does not receive any potion.

Jonas is holding a Potion of Generosity.
Mika, holding a Potion of Joy and a Potion of Empathy, accepted to trade with Jonas.
Tonight Jonas will receive the Potion of Joy and Empathy from Mika and Mika will receive the Potion of Generosity.

Mulan is holding a Potion of Stress and a Potion of Terror.
Taki, who is holding a Potion of Generosity and a Potion of Disdain, accepted to trade with Mulan.
Tonight one of the evil Potions (Potion of Stress, Terror or Disdain) will break together with the Potion of Generosity from Taki.
The storyteller decides to destroy the Potion of Terror.
Afterward Taki received the Potion of Stress from Mulan, who receives a Potion of Disdain from Taki.

Karol accepted to trade his Potion of Generosity with Moll holding a Potion of Panic.
Tonight Karol receives the Potion of Panic and Moll the Potion of Generosity, since Potion of Panic is neither good nor evil.
