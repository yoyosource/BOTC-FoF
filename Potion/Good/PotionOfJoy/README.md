# Potion of Joy
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> If the demon kills you at night, a number of players equal to the number of living evil players receive a good potion.

*Sometimes confused with Potion of Alcohol.*

This potion can help the good team gauge how many evil players are still alive and thus in play.
It furthermore can help the game balance out an overwhelming evil majority by helping the good team with good potions.

## How to run
When a player is killed by the demon at night holding a Potion of Joy the storyteller gives out *n* player a good potion.
*n* is defined by the number of alive evil players.

## Examples
Albin the Demon is killing Jennifer holding a Potion of Joy.
Since there are no other evil players alive only one person receives a good potion.

Paul was killed by the Demon tonight.
He was holding a Potion of Joy.
There are one evil Outsider, one Minion as well as the Demon still alive.
This is why three players received a good potion this night.
