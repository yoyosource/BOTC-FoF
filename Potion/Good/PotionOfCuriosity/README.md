# Potion of Curiosity
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each day you may break this Potion with the Storyteller privately to learn a player you have something in common with.

*WARNING: Not safe for cat consumption.*

The Potion of Curiosity lends its holder the ability to gain a bit more information as long as they can figure out what the connection is.

## How to run

Once per day, if a player holding a Potion of Curiosity requests a private chat with you, take them away from the circle so you cannot be overheard.
Whisper them a player that they have something in common with, WITHOUT disclosing the similarity.
(If you cannot think of a player straight away, either take your time or ask them to come back in a few minutes.)
Afterward remove the Potion of Curiosity from the player.

## Examples

George the Student asks the Storyteller for a private consultation, in which he asks to break his Potion of Curiosity to use its ability.
If he is holding a Potion of Curiosity at this time, the Storyteller removes it and names Lily, without revealing that Lily and he has in common that both their characters' names start with the same letter, as Lily is the Scout.

Jonas uses a Potion of Curiosity and learns Marcus.
This is because both have an Outsider as their neighbour.

Ronald uses his Potion of Curiosity and learns Emma.
This is because both Emma and Ronald traded with an evil player last night.

Christopher uses his Potion of Curiosity and learns Matthias.
Both players have in common that they do not wake during the night due to their own abilities.

Joe visits the Storyteller and asks to use their Potion of Curiosity.
As they are not holding a Potion of Curiosity at the time, the Storyteller simply denies the request without further clarification.

Yanis visits the Storyteller and asks to use their Potion of Curiosity.
As the Potion of Curiosity they are holding at the time is nullified, the Storyteller simply denies the request without further clarification.
