# Potion of Conviction
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> This potion cannot be nullified. You may publicly break this potion to nominate a player that was previously nominated today even if you have nominated today.

*I'm quite certain drinking this was a good choice!*

The Potion of Conviction gives its user another shot at nominating a player they believe should be executed today, even though they have been nominated previously today.

## How to run
Potion of Conviction CANNOT be nullified by any means!

Any player can publicly (during the nomination phase) say that they want to use a Potion of Conviction to nominate a player that was already nominated today (even if you have nominated someone today).
If they have a Potion of Conviction facilitate this nomination as any other nomination, otherwise nothing happens.

## Examples

Linda wishes to nominate Carl, but Carl was nominated earlier today by Stephen.
Linda publicly asks the Storyteller to break her Potion of Conviction in order to make that nomination happen.
The Storyteller removes the Potion of Conviction from Linda and announces that Linda has nominated Carl.

Mulan was nominated today by Lisa and was not put on the block.
Afterward Caro decides that to use a Potion of Conviction, which she does not hold, to nominate Mulan a second time.
The storyteller acknowledges the attempt, but does not announce a nomination.
