# Potion of Obsession
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When this potion is unveiled; until dusk, the number of votes needed to be marked for death is increased by one. This potion breaks.

*I won't forget, I CAN'T forget. It nestles in my mind like a vicious parasite, clouding my thoughts on all but the thoughts of what could have been. Only, if only, I could turn back time, re-write history a million times, until what was wronged is right once more.*

The Potion of Obsession acts as an obstacle for executions, demanding of the players to be more coherent in their decisions than ordinarily.

## How to run
During the day when a Potion of Obsession is unveiled mark them with the **OBSESSED** reminder and remove the Potion of Obsession.

Before nomination count up the number of **OBSESSED** reminders present.
This number defines how many extra votes are needed to be marked for execution but do not announce that this is the case.
Just say if the person is now on the block or not (with the extra votes in mind).

At dusk remove any **OBSESSED** reminders.

## Examples

Richard asks the Storyteller to unveil his potions, and upon doing so a Potion of Obsession is unveiled.
The storyteller puts the **OBSESSED** reminder token on Richard and removes the Potion of Obsession.
During nominations today, the number of votes needed to mark a player for death is increased by 1.

Kenny asks the Storyteller to unveil his potions, and upon doing so a Potion of Obsession is unveiled.
The storyteller puts the **OBSESSED** reminder token on Kenny.
The same day, Lina does the same thing with the same result.
The storyteller puts the **OBSESSED** reminder token on Lina.
During nominations today, the number of votes needed to mark a player for death is increased by 2.
