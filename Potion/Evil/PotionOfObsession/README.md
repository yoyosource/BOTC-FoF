# Potion of Obsession
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When this potion is revealed; until dusk, the number of votes needed to be marked for death is increased by one. This potion breaks.

*I won't forget, I CAN'T forget. It nestles in my mind like a vicious parasite, clouding my thoughts on all but the thoughts of what could have been. Only, if only, I could turn back time, re-write history a million times, until what was wronged is right once more.*
