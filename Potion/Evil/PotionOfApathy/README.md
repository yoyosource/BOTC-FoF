# Potion of Apathy
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> If you are targeted by a player, they might receive an evil potion. Then this potion might break.

*...*

The Potion of Apathy is a source of evil potions being spread across town.

## How to run
When a player is targeted by any ability and are holding a Potion of Apathy,
decide if you want to give the player targeting them an evil potion. If you do,  put any evil potion on them.
Then decide if you want to remove the Potion of Apathy.

## Examples
Marcus is holding a Potion of Apathy and is targeted by Lilly the Chemist.
The storyteller decides to give an evil potion (e.g. Potion of Stress) to Lilly but do not remove the Potion of Apathy.
Note that the Potion of Apathy happens instantaneously, and as Lilly is actively looking at the Cauldron when they make their choice they see the potion added to them at this time.

Mika is holding a Potion of Apathy and is targeted by Jonas the Druid.
The storyteller decided to neither give out a potion nor break the Potion of Apathy.

Carlo the Baptist targets Christopher holding a Potion of Apathy.
The storyteller decided to remove the Potion of Apathy without giving an evil potion to Carlo.

Jennifer the Thug targets Marcus holding a Potion of Apathy.
The storyteller decided to give an evil potion (e.g. Potion of Terror) to Jennifer and remove the Potion of Apathy from Marcus.

Alex is holding a Potion of Apathy and is nominated by Gertrude.
Nominations are NOT targeting effects, and therefor the Potion of Apathy cannot trigger.

John the Tramp's ability is used by the Storyteller to give a Potion of Panic to Moll, who is holding a Potion of Panic.
As the Tramp didn't make a selection, the Potion of Apathy cannot trigger.
