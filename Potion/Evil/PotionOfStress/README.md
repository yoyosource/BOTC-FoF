# Potion of Stress
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> This potion might break at any time, except the night it was received, to have the holder register falsely until dawn.

*Some say it's just caffeinated water.*

The Potion of Stress causes misregistration, but with a chance for discovery ahead of time.

## How to run
The storyteller can break this potion to register the person holding it falsely (e.g. character, character type, and or alignment, ...).

If this potion was received tonight the storyteller cannot break this potion to cause misregistration.

## Examples

The 1st night of the game Joshua receives a Potion of Stress from the Glassblower.
When the Scout's turn in the Night Order comes up, the Storyteller CANNOT break the Potion of Stress to make him misregister to the Scout's ability, as the Potion of Stress was received the same night.

Nico the Distiller is holding a Potion of Stress and receives a Potion of Empathy.
When the Clerk's turn comes in the night order the storyteller breaks Nico's Potion of Stress and the Clerk learns that an evil player received a Potion of Empathy tonight due to the misregistration.

Jonas is holding a Potion of Stress and agreed to trade with Marcus who is holding a Potion of Panic.
Tonight the storyteller breaks the Potion of Stress to its own ability before the trade happens, then the trade happens and Jonas receives the Potion of Panic.
Marcus does not receive any Potion.
Later that night the Storyteller may misregister Jonas's alignment to the Auctioneer due to the Potion of Stress's ability.
