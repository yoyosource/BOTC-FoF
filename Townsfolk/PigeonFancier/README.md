# Pigeon Fancier
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> If you died at night, you learn an evil player.

*Fancy a pigeon?*

The Pigeon Fancier is a demon trap, a Townsfolk that tilts the game in good's favor if killed at night.

## How to run
When the Pigeon Fancier was killed during the night wake them and point to a player that is evil.

## Examples
Rita the Caetrum chooses Mark to die at night.
Mark is the Pigeon Fancier, so this night Mark learns Rita the Caetrum.

Jonny the Pigeon Fancier was executed during the day.
Nothing happens.

## Tips & Tricks

- You only learn an evil player if you die at night, so your ability is a trap sprung on the demon rather than something you can trigger yourself.
- You get nothing if you are executed during the day, so try to stay off the block and let the demon come to you.
- You can still talk as a dead player, so once you die be ready to tell the town who you learned the moment you get the chance.
- Making yourself look like a threat can tempt the demon into killing you, which is exactly when your ability pays off.

## Bluffing as the Pigeon Fancier

- While alive the Pigeon Fancier does nothing, so it is one of the lowest-maintenance bluffs on the script - no night wakes and no daily information to fake.
- Claiming it signals you are a harmless townsfolk the demon might as well ignore, which can quietly keep you alive.
- You have nothing to prove until you die, so your claim cannot be checked or contradicted during the day.
- If you are an evil player who does die at night, you can claim the Pigeon Fancier and name a good player as the evil one you learned, throwing suspicion where you want it.
- As the demon, consider killing your own Minion who is claiming the Pigeon Fancier, so they "learn" an evil player at night and can name a good player, lending the whole evil team credibility.
- Because a real Pigeon Fancier produces nothing when executed, being voted out does not expose your bluff at all.
