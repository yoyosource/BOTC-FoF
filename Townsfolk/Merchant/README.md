# Merchant
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When you trade either player receives an additional potion. An evil potion might become a good potion.

*I will buy with you, sell with you, talk with you, walk with you, and so following, but I will not eat with you, drink with you, nor pray with you.*

The Merchant can potentially confirm itself by using the Trade mechanic, and also replace evil potions in town with good ones.

## How to run
Each time the Merchant traded with a player, decide if the Merchant or the trade partner receives an additional Potion.
If any evil Potion was part of the trade, decide if an evil Potion is exchanged by a good Potion.

## Examples
Kilian the Merchant traded with Marcus.
Kilian received an additional Potion of Obsession from the trade, besides the already traded Potions.

Vincent the Merchant traded with Jorick.
Vincent receives a Potion of Generosity through their own ability.
Jorick was holding a Potion of Disdain.
Jorick's Potion can now be exchanged into any type of good Potion, and was exchanged into a Potion of Curiosity, now held by Marcus.
