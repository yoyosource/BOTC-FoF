# Merchant
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> When you trade either player receives an additional potion. An evil potion might become a good potion.

*I will buy with you, sell with you, talk with you, walk with you, and so following, but I will not eat with you, drink with you, nor pray with you.*

The Merchant can potentially confirm itself by using the Trade mechanic, and also replace evil potions in town with good ones.

## How to run
Each time the Merchant traded with a player, decide if the Merchant or the trade partner receives an additional Potion.
If any evil Potion was part of the trade, decide if an evil Potion is exchanged by a good Potion.

## Examples
Kilian the Merchant traded with Marcus.
Kilian received an additional Potion of Obsession from the trade, besides the already traded Potions.

Vincent the Merchant traded with Jorick.
Vincent receives a Potion of Generosity through their own ability.
Jorick was holding a Potion of Disdain.
Jorick's Potion can now be exchanged into any type of good Potion, and was exchanged into a Potion of Curiosity, now held by Marcus.

## Tips & Tricks

- Every time you trade, one of the two of you gains an extra potion, and if an evil potion was part of the trade it might turn good.
- Trading is public, so trading with a player you trust and both watching the extra potion appear is a strong way to help confirm you.
- A fair exchange leaves no evil behind: trade with players holding evil potions to launder them into good ones and clean up the board.
- The more you trade, the more value you generate, so be an active and willing trading partner rather than sitting on your potions.
- The Storyteller decides who gets the extra potion and whether an evil potion turns good, so you propose the trade but cannot fully dictate the result.

## Bluffing as the Merchant

- Your effect shows up in the Cauldron as extra potions appearing and evil potions turning good, so keep your claims consistent with what the trades actually produce.
- Trade with a teammate and take credit for the Merchant bonus to help confirm a false Merchant between the two of you.
- The evil-to-good swap is a convenient story for an evil potion that quietly leaves the Cauldron, letting you look like you cleaned it up.
- Lean on evil teammates like the Commira or Thug placing potions at night, and the Chemist moving or clearing potions in the Cauldron, to keep the board busy enough that your claimed bonuses blend in.
- Being a keen trader also lets you move potions around your own team while wearing a helpful, town-friendly face.
