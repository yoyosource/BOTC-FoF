# Toll Inspector
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night*, eye the cauldron. [You choose your slot on the night order.]

*I had to pay a ransom of six hundred pounds in gold. All might have gone well even yet, only that, by ins and outs and crookedness of laws, I was shorn like a sheep that is clipped to the quick.*

## How to run
During setup/first spot in the first night, wake up the Toll Inspector.
Let them choose to either be before another character in the night order or after one.
Put them back to sleep.
On your own night order Sheet mark the Toll Inspector to be woken before/after the named character.

Each night wake up the Toll Inspector.
Let them see the Cauldron for a bit.
Put the Toll Inspector back to sleep.

## Examples
Gertrude the Toll Inspector wakes at the beginning of the 1st night and chooses the Druid & Before on the Other Night order sheet.
From night 2 onwards, the Toll Inspector wakes up just before a Druid would wake up and is shown the Cauldron.

## Tips & Tricks

- Eyeing the Cauldron at night shows you every evil potion unveiled, so you can see which players are holding evil potions and track how that changes from night to night.
- Your real power is choosing your night-order slot: waking just after a suspected potion-giver lets you see what they added, while waking earlier snapshots the Cauldron before it is stirred.
- Ins and outs are the whole game here, so pick a slot next to a character whose effect you most want to catch - sit after a heavy potion-distributor, or after a mover or breaker like the Chemist or Toxicologist to see the aftermath.
- A potion appearing on, or vanishing from, a player right around a character's turn is a strong pointer to what that character did.
- Eye the Cauldron again during the day and compare it to your night view: if potions have moved, appeared, or broken since your slot, something acted after you woke - even though daytime potions are veiled, their count and positions still show that a change happened.
- What you see in the Cauldron is the real board state, not storyteller-given information, so being drunk or poisoned does not change the potions shown to you.

## Bluffing as the Toll Inspector

- Your information is only "I looked at the Cauldron", so it is easy to claim you saw whatever suits your team.
- The Cauldron is semi-public and any player can eye it during the day, so keep your claimed observations consistent with what others can also see for themselves.
- Commit to a night-order slot and stick to it; claiming a potion appeared at a time that does not match who actually gives potions can be caught out.
- Anchor your lies to potions that really exist in the Cauldron, since the board is public - contradict the veiled potions everyone can count and you are exposed.
- As an evil player you may want to under-report or misattribute evil potions you would really have seen, to protect the teammates holding them.
- Alternatively, honestly report an evil potion on a teammate you are willing to sacrifice, spending real information to build yourself up as a reliable Toll Inspector.
- The Toll Inspector only eyes the Cauldron from the second night, so do not claim a first-night read.
