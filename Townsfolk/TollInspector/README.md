# Toll Inspector
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night*, eye the cauldron. [You choose your slot on the night order.]

*I had to pay a ransom of six hundred pounds in gold. All might have gone well even yet, only that, by ins and outs and crookedness of laws, I was shorn like a sheep that is clipped to the quick.*

## How to run
During setup/first spot in the first night, wake up the Toll Inspector.
Let them choose to either be before another character in the night order or after one.
Put them back to sleep.
On your own night order Sheet mark the Toll Inspector to be woken before/after the named character.

Each night wake up the Toll Inspector.
Let them see the Cauldron for a bit.
Put the Toll Inspector back to sleep.

## Examples
Gertrude the Toll Inspector wakes at the beginning of the 1st night and chooses the Druid & Before on the Other Night order sheet.
From night 2 onwards, the Toll Inspector wakes up just before a Druid would wake up and is shown the Cauldron.
