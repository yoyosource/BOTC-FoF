# Botanist
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> You start knowing 2 good characters; 1 is in play and 1 is a demon bluff.

*Man, that's groovy.*

The Botanist gives the good team a good starting point of information, allowing them to identify potential evil players.

## How to run
During the 1st night, wake the Botanist.
Show them 2 good characters.
1 is in play and the other is a Demon bluff.

## Examples
Mike the Botanist wakes up on the 1st night and learns Auctioneer and Merchant.
Olivia is the Merchant, and the Auctioneer is one of the Demon bluffs.

## Tips & Tricks

- Both characters you learn are good ones: exactly one is genuinely in play and the other is a Demon bluff, but you are not told which is which.
- Anyone who claims one of your two characters is either the real one in play or an evil player leaning on that bluff, so dig into both claimants rather than trusting either on sight.
- If one of your characters is never claimed, it is either a quiet or dead honest player or an unused Demon bluff - either way you have narrowed down the Demon's likely disguises.
- Your pair only names good characters, so it says nothing directly about who is evil; treat it as a shortlist of claims to scrutinise.
- Groovy as your information feels, it can be wrong if you were drunk or poisoned on the first night, for example by a veiled Potion of Panic or a Potion of Stress.

## Bluffing as the Botanist

- The Botanist only acts on the first night, so claiming it never forces you to invent ongoing night information.
- Name two good characters, ideally one you believe is genuinely in play and one you want the town to distrust.
- A real Botanist may hold a different pair, so expect your claim to be cross-checked and avoid a combination an honest Botanist could not plausibly have been shown.
- Avoid naming a character your own team is already bluffing, as putting a teammate's disguise on your list can validate or expose them.
- Conversely, you can name a character a teammate is bluffing on purpose, lending their claim weight by implying to the town that it is the good character genuinely in play.
- As the Demon, the Botanist is comfortable cover for never waking, but keep your two named characters consistent if you are questioned on them later.
