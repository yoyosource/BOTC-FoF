# Botanist
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> You start knowing 2 good characters; 1 is in play and 1 is a demon bluff.

*Man, that's groovy.*

The Botanist gives the good team a good starting point of information, allowing them to identify potential evil players.

## How to run
During the 1st night, wake the Botanist.
Show them 2 good characters.
1 is in play and the other is a Demon bluff.

## Examples
Mike the Botanist wakes up on the 1st night and learns Auctioneer and Merchant.
Olivia is the Merchant, and the Auctioneer is one of the Demon bluffs.

## Tips & Tricks
- As you start out, keep what roles you learned secret. If you only find a single player claiming either role, it most likely means that the bluff is not being used and that player is good.
- If you find claims of both characters, you can out your information and push for both of the players' death, in order to guarantee the death of an evil player.
- Alternatively, pay close attention to their behavior. Do not out your information and try to use it to deduce which players they seem to be working together with in order to catch more evil players.

## Bluffing as the Botanist
- If you know one of the demon bluffs, that nobody is using, use that with another demon bluff to make an evil player trustworthy.
- Alternatively, claim to know the roles of two claimed, in-play good characters, in order to frame either as being evil with a bluff.
- Avoiding your known demon bluffs can help a teammate to blend into the game easier.
