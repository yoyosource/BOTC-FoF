# Farrier
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night, you learn a good player. There is an evil player that registers as good to you.

*Well, that is close. But close only counts for hand grenades.*

The Farrier is a powerful character, helping confirm their fellow teammates. However, 1 evil player might be part of that confirmation.

## How to run
Put the 'Blue Herring' reminder token onto an evil player.

Each night wake up the Farrier.
Point towards a good player or the 'Blue Herring'.
Put the Farrier back to sleep.

## Examples
Brain the Farrier wakes up and is shown the player Dusty.
Dusty is a good player.

Ben the Farrier wakes up and is to learn a good player.
As Marcus the Megalomaniac has the 'Blue Herring' reminder token on him, he registers as good to
the Farrier, and thus Ben can and does learn Marcus tonight.

## Tips & Tricks

- Each night you learn a player who registers as good to you, so over several nights you build up a pool of players you can lean towards trusting.
- Exactly one evil player is your Blue Herring and registers as good, so close only counts here too - any single night's learn could be that one false positive.
- The more different players you are shown, the more diluted your Blue Herring becomes; a player corroborated by other information is safer to trust than a lone read.
- Do not treat your learns as hard confirmations, and share them carefully, as loudly vouching for your Blue Herring hands the evil team a shield.
- Your read for a night can also be wrong if you were drunk or poisoned, for example by a veiled Potion of Panic or a Potion of Stress, on top of the Blue Herring.

## Bluffing as the Farrier

- The Farrier wakes every night and produces a steady stream of good players, making it a flexible bluff for vouching for teammates or steering the town's trust.
- Name players who are plausibly good, and consider confirming an evil teammate to lend them cover, which the Blue Herring conveniently explains in-fiction.
- Do not over-confirm; a real Farrier stays cautious because of the Blue Herring, so claiming total certainty about everyone you name rings false.
- Because you wake nightly, keep track of who you have already learned so your story stays consistent from one day to the next.
- Slow-roll your reveal like a genuine Farrier gathering data over time, rather than dumping a full list of confirmed players at once.
