# Connoisseur
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each day, you may visit the storyteller to learn a player that is a non-neutral execution for your team today.

*Everything in life that’s any fun, as somebody wisely observed, is either immoral, illegal or fattening.*

The Connoisseur learns information that they can interpret to find out either particularly useful or especially detrimental players.

## How to run
When the Connoisseur asks for their information tell them a player that, if they were to be executed, would be either useful or detrimental for their team.

## Examples
Connor the Connoisseur goes to the storyteller to receive their information.
You tell them Jade the Megalomaniac, which would be a very good execution for Connor's team.

Connan the Connoisseur in turn learns Marcus the Apothecary.
Marcus would be a detrimental execution for Connan's team, as the Apothecary is currently nullifying a dangerous type of potion.

## Tips & Tricks
- Make sure to visit the Storyteller each day for your information, and try to get a chat in with the player you learned in order to deduce if you think their claims makes sense with them being learned.
- Pay attention to the actions of the person/persons you've learned; evil players are rarely considered neutral executions, so suspicious behavior together with being learned by you might be enough to get an evil out of the way!
- Remember that the information you learn is subjective, and given to you from the Storyteller's point of view. Always be open to the possibility of an incorrect interpretation.

## Bluffing as the Connoisseur
- Since you learn your information in private by visiting the storyteller do so on days you want to claim to have learned a player.
- Learning a player does not include if that player is a good execution or a bad one. You can put your own read into whatever your claim.
- Naming a teammate and warning the town that they have a good role can keep them alive longer.
- Alternatively name a known good player and push town into thinking they are evil.
- Late in the game, claim to have learned yourself. This might instill panic in the good team, as this would imply that maybe you are one of the last good players left alive.
