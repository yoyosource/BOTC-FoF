# Connoisseur
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each day, you may visit the storyteller to learn a player that is a non-neutral execution for your team today.

*Everything in life that’s any fun, as somebody wisely observed, is either immoral, illegal or fattening.*

The Connoisseur learns information that they can interpret to find out either particularly useful or especially detrimental players.

## How to run
When the Connoisseur asks for their information tell them a player that, if they were to be executed, would be either useful or detrimental for their team.

## Examples
Connor the Connoisseur goes to the storyteller to receive their information.
You tell them Jade the Megalomaniac, which would be a very good execution for Connor's team.

Connan the Connoisseur in turn learns Marcus the Apothecary.
Marcus would be a detrimental execution for Connan's team, as the Apothecary is currently nullifying a dangerous type of potion.
