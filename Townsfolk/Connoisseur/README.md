# Connoisseur
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each day, you may visit the storyteller to learn a player that is a non-neutral execution for your team today.

*Everything in life that’s any fun, as somebody wisely observed, is either immoral, illegal or fattening.*

The Connoisseur learns information that they can interpret to find out either particularly useful or especially detrimental players.

## How to run
When the Connoisseur asks for their information tell them a player that, if they were to be executed, would be either useful or detrimental for their team.

## Examples
Connor the Connoisseur goes to the storyteller to receive their information.
You tell them Jade the Megalomaniac, which would be a very good execution for Connor's team.

Connan the Connoisseur in turn learns Marcus the Apothecary.
Marcus would be a detrimental execution for Connan's team, as the Apothecary is currently nullifying a dangerous type of potion.

## Tips & Tricks

- Each day you may ask the Storyteller for a player whose execution today would matter for the good team, but you are not told whether executing them helps or hurts.
- The named player is either someone worth executing, such as the demon or a dangerous minion, or someone you badly want to keep alive, such as a powerful townsfolk.
- It is up to you to work out which: read the named player's claim and behaviour to judge whether putting them on the block is a win or a trap.
- The read is about today, so ask on a day you actually intend to swing an execution, and ask again on later days as the game state changes.
- Sharing the name without a firm read can backfire, since the town might execute a player you were meant to protect - be careful how you present it.
- Your information can be wrong if you were drunk or poisoned, for example by a veiled Potion of Panic or a Potion of Stress, so treat it as a lead rather than a certainty.

## Bluffing as the Connoisseur

- Your information is private and ambiguous, so you can claim to have learned any player and spin them as either a good execution or one to avoid.
- Even a real Connoisseur does not know whether their read is useful or detrimental, so your interpretation is almost impossible to falsify.
- Name a good player and push for their execution as a "useful" read, or name a teammate and warn the town off executing them as a "detrimental" one.
- You have no night action and only ever visit the Storyteller, so it is a low-maintenance claim that never risks a mistimed wake.
- Refresh your claimed read on later days to look like a genuine Connoisseur re-checking the changing game state.
