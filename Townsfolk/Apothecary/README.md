# Apothecary
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> All types of potions you are holding are nullified.

*Medicine is not only a science; it is also an art. It does not consist of compounding pills and plasters; it deals with the very processes of life, which must be understood before they may be guided.*

The Apothecary allows the Storyteller to effectively cancel out types of Potions, with the Apothecary being able to identify them.
This allows the character to confirm themselves and help the good team counteract negative effects.

## How to run
While the Apothecary holds potions, potions of those types have no abilities.

## Examples
Joris the Apothecary is holding a Potion of Curiosity.
Jonas tries to use his Potion of Curiosity, they are told that they cannot.

Moll the Apothecary is holding a Potion of Panic.
Potion of Panic cannot be nullified, thus an unveiled Potion of Panic will stay unveiled, even if a Potion of Panic is held by the Apothecary.

Marcus the Apothecary is holding a Potion of Joy.
At night the Caetrum targets Marcus, and he dies.
At the time the Potion of Joy breaks, it was held by a living Apothecary, thus it was nullified and does not trigger.

## Tips & Tricks

- Every type of potion you hold has its ability switched off across the whole game, not just your own copy, so holding one Potion of Terror nullifies every Potion of Terror in play.
- The cure for a bad potion is to hold one yourself: get a copy of a dangerous evil potion type into your hands and every potion of that type goes quiet.
- To identify the types you are nullifying, eye the Cauldron and unveil your own potions so you can see exactly what you hold.
- Eye the Cauldron but leave your own potions veiled: a Potion of Panic cannot be nullified and stays unveiled for good once flipped, and a Potion of Terror breaks and helps the evil team when unveiled even though you are nullifying it.
- Or do not look at all: your potions are nullified whether or not you know what they are, so you may simply not need to find out.
- Prioritise holding the nastiest evil types; a good potion in your hands is wasted, since you would be switching off helpful potions too.
- Potion of Panic cannot be nullified, so never rely on holding one to shut it down.
- Trades and friendly potion-givers are your best way to pick up a specific type you want to cancel.

## Bluffing as the Apothecary

- Whether a potion is actually nullified is not visible to the town, so you can claim to be shutting down whichever type you name.
- Claim to be nullifying a scary evil potion type to look like a helpful townsfolk protecting the board.
- Your held potions still show in the Cauldron and evil ones unveil at night, so keep your claimed type consistent with what you are really holding.
- Claiming to know which types are in play lets you invent believable information about the potion pool.
- If a potion someone relied on fails for any reason, you can take credit and say your nullification is what stopped it.
