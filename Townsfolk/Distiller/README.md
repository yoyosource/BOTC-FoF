# Distiller
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night*, choose 2 alive players; 1 receives a good potion or 1 of their evil potions break.

*Ein Glas ist fabelhaft, zwei sind zu viel, und drei zu wenig.*

The Distiller acts primarily as a Good source of potions for the town, with the addition of being able to remove evil potions from play.

## How to run
Each night except the first, wake the Distiller and have them choose 2 living players.
Put them back to sleep.

You as the storyteller can now choose if either player receives 1 good Potion or if 1 of the evil Potions either player is holding breaks.
If neither player holds an evil Potion you must give a good Potion to either player.

## Examples
Joshua the Distiller chooses themselves and Mary. 
Mary is currently holding a Potion of Obsession.
The Storyteller chooses for Joshua to receive a Potion of Joy.

Leon the Distiller choose Albin and Jake.
Albin holds a Potion of Conviction while Jake is holding a Potion of Apathy.
The storyteller chooses to remove the Potion of Apathy from Jake.

Carlo the Distiller chooses Marcus and Jonas.
Neither Marcus nor Jonas are holding any Potions.
The storyteller must give out a good Potion to either player.
They choose Marcus to receive a Potion of Joy.

## Tips & Tricks

- Each night you pick 2 living players and the Storyteller either gives one of them a good potion or breaks one evil potion they hold; you propose the pair, but the Storyteller decides the outcome.
- One good glass a night makes you a reliable source of good potions, so point your picks at players you trust to get beneficial potions into the right hands.
- You may include yourself, which is a clean way to build a good potion onto yourself and help prove your claim.
- Pairing two players you suspect are holding evil potions gives the Storyteller the option to break one, letting you quietly cleanse the board.
- Because the Storyteller chooses which effect happens, you cannot guarantee the result, so watch the Cauldron afterwards to see whether a potion appeared or broke and on whom.
- If neither of your picks holds an evil potion, a good potion must be handed out, so pairing two empty-handed trusted players guarantees a good potion lands.

## Bluffing as the Distiller

- Your effect is visible as potions appearing or breaking in the Cauldron, so keep your claimed picks in line with what actually happens on the board.
- Claiming the Distiller lets you take credit for good potions that show up, whoever really placed them, which builds trust and muddies the real potion sources.
- Your own evil team can manufacture your cover: characters like the Commira and Thug place potions on players at night, giving you fresh potions to claim as your own distilling.
- Coordinate with those teammates so a good potion they place looks like your handiwork, letting you take the credit and the trust while they do the work.
- The Commira is especially good cover, since it can hand several players a potion in a single night, so there is always something fresh to explain away as yours.
- Do not claim to have broken an evil potion that is still sitting unbroken in the Cauldron.
- You can name trusted good players as your targets to look helpful while your real evil potions land elsewhere.
- The Distiller wakes from the second night, so you have no first-night action to claim.
