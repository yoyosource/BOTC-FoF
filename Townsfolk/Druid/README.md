# Druid
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night* you may choose a dead player; learn their character type and you might receive an evil potion.

*The greatest use of life is to spend it for something that will outlast it.*

The Druid confirms dead players as the character type they are, at the risk of being saddled with an Evil potion.

## How to run
Each night except the first, wake the Druid and let them choose a dead player.
Tell the Druid the character type the dead player is (Townsfolk, Outsider, Minion, Demon).
Put the Druid back to sleep.

Decide if the Druid should receive an evil Potion based on the information they received, if yes give the Druid an evil Potion.

## Examples
Sandra the Druid wakes up on the 2nd night and chooses Karen the dead Scout.
Sandra learns that Karen was a Townsfolk character.
The Storyteller then chooses for Sandra to receive a Potion of Terror.

Cleo the Druid wakes up and picks Marcus.
Marcus is not dead, so Cleo is told to pick another player.

Ben the Druid wakes up on night 2 and is offered to choose a player.
Ben decides to not choose a player and is then put back to sleep.
The Storyteller may NOT put an Evil potion on Ben since they did not choose a player.

Basti the Druid wakes up and chooses Leo the dead Crone.
Basti is told that Leo was a Minion and is then put back to sleep.
The Storyteller decides to put a Potion of Obsession on Basti.

## Tips & Tricks

- Each night from the second on you may choose a dead player and learn their character type - Townsfolk, Outsider, Minion, or Demon.
- Even the dead have their uses: confirming an executed or killed player's type lets you check whether their claim held up.
- You only learn the type, not the exact character, so pair it with what that player claimed in life to confirm them or expose a lie.
- The choice is optional and only picking a player risks an evil potion, so you can skip a night to stay clean when you do not need the information.
- The more valuable the read turns out to be, the more likely the Storyteller hands you an evil potion, so a bigger reveal tends to come at a price.
- Any evil potion you gain sits in the Cauldron working against you, so plan to trade it away or have it broken by a friendly Distiller or Toxicologist.

## Bluffing as the Druid

- Your information is private, so it is easy to claim you learned whatever character type suits your team.
- Claim a dead teammate came back as Townsfolk to launder their alignment, or claim a dead good player was a Minion to cast doubt on those who trusted them.
- The Druid genuinely risks receiving evil potions, so it is the perfect cover for any evil potion you are actually holding - just say you got it from Druiding.
- Claim you chose no one on nights you would rather not commit to a piece of information.
- Your killers, like the Commira and Caetrum, keep making fresh corpses for you to "read", so there is always a believable target for your nightly claim.
