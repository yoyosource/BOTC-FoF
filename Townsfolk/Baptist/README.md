# Baptist
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night*, choose 2 living players (not yourself); either player might receive a potion. You cannot die on nights players received evil potions from you.

*It's never too soon to contemplate eternal damnation, sir!*

The Baptist acts as a Good source of potions, with the potential of denying the demon a kill in the night.

## How to run
Each night except the first, wake the Baptist and have them choose 2 living players.
Put them back to sleep.

You as the storyteller chooses if neither, either or both receive a Potion.
If you choose an evil Potion for either player, put the 'Cannot die' reminder onto the Baptist.

If the Baptist trades potions with a player, and the potions they trade away includes an evil potion, put the 'Cannot die' reminder token on the Baptist.
This is true for any other interaction that moves an evil potion from the Baptist to another player.

## Examples
Richard the Baptist wakes up and chooses Tom and Lisa.
The Storyteller puts a Potion of Stress on Lisa and a Potion of Joy on Tom.
Then, as another player has received an evil potion from Richard tonight, 
the Storyteller puts the 'Cannot die' reminder token on Richard.

Jakob the Baptist wakes up and chooses Marcus and Marvin.
The Storyteller put a Potion of Joy on Marcus and no Potion on Marvin.
The Baptist will not be protected from kills this night.

Moritz the Baptist has traded their Potions with Leo.
Moritz held a Potion of Terror while Leo did not have any Potions.
Since another player received an evil Potion by the Baptist the storyteller puts the 'Cannot die' reminder token on Moritz.

The Chemist chooses to trade the Potion of Apathy from Nicklas with the Potion of Empathy from Nico.
The storyteller puts the 'Cannot die' reminder on Nicklas the Baptist.

Freya and Lina announces that they wish to trade potions during the day.
The Storyteller puts the 'Trade X' Reminder token on them.
The following night, Freya and Lina exchanges potions.
Freya the Baptist was holding a Potion of Apathy, and Lina was holding a Potion of Generosity.
Once the potions have swapped places, the Potion of Generosity triggers, breaking the Potion of Apathy.
Since the evil potion held by the Baptist was received by another player, 
the Storyteller puts the 'Cannot die' reminder token on Freya.
