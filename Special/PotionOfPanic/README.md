# Potion of Panic
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Cannot be nullified. This potion cannot be veiled once unveiled. When broken, if veiled, the Megalomaniac acts twice next night.

*Has a somewhat greenish-teal hue, unlike the Potion of Pain which is a pinkish purple.*

The Potion of Panic is a Potion that acts as a special interactive token for a few, select characters on the script. Its purpose is solely to interact with those characters.

## How to run
Potion of Panic CANNOT be nullified by any means!

Once the Potion of Panic is unveiled do not veil it again.

Each night* if a veiled Potion of Panic broke last night or today, tonight the Megalomaniac acts twice.

If multiple veiled Potions of Panic has broken since last night, the Megalomaniac still only acts twice tonight.

## Examples
Jonas asked the storyteller to unveil their Potions and unveiled a Potion of Panic with that.
The storyteller veils all potions except the Potion of Panic.
During nomination Jonas was executed by town.
Tonight Marcus the Megalomaniac does not act twice because the Potion of Panic destroyed was unveiled at the time.

Stefan was nominated and executed by town today and was holding a veiled Potion of Panic.
Tonight Oliver the Megalomaniac will act twice because the Potion of Panic was destroyed while veiled.

Jerry the Apothecary asks to have their potions unveiled and a Potion of Panic is unveiled.
The Potion of Panic cannot be nullified by the Apothecary's ability, and therefor does not become veiled again.
