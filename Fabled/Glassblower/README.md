# Glassblower

Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night, potions equal to the voting majority minus 1 are given out.

*Sometimes, the most beautiful creations are born from shattered dreams.*

## How to run

When it is the Glassblower's time in the night order, calculate the following:\
Living Players divided by two (rounded up), and then subtract one. (e.g. 10: ⌈10/2⌉-1 = 4)\
Select that many Potions for players to receive.

## Examples

There are 10 players still alive in this round. Using the calculation you figure out that 4 potions should be given out.
As Victor is not holding any potions right now. So you decide to give them a Potion of Terror.
Jacob is holding a Potion of Curiosity. So you decide to balance that by giving them a Potion of Apathy.
Sarah is the Demon and you decide that they should get a Potion of Empathy to help them with their decisions.
Juan has not had many potions this game, and you decide to give them a Potion of Generosity.
Now that you have given out 4 potions continue with the next step in the night order.
