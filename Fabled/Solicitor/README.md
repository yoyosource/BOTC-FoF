# Solicitor

Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> 2 living players may publicly choose to trade all potions once per day. If either player is dead when the trade would happen, no trade occurs.

*Oh, I love the Festival! It's my busiest workday of the year!*

The Solicitor is used for players to be able to add interactions to the Potions even outside specific character abilities and facilitates the Trade mechanic.

## How to run

Each day use the reminder tokens to mark down any Trades players announce that they wish to make.
The following night, starting with Trade 1, move all potion reminders from one side of each trade to the other side of
each trade and vice versa.
If either player in a trade is dead, remove the trade reminder token from both parties and do not perform that trade.

## Examples

Jenny asks Graham publicly if they wish to trade. Graham says "yes".
You put down **Trade 1** reminder token next to Graham and Jenny on the Grimoire and confirm that this Trade is happening.
The following night, as both Jenny and Graham are still alive, move all potions from Jenny to Graham and vice versa.
Remove the **Trade 1** reminder token from both Jenny and Graham.

Mathew and Marcus publicly decide to make a trade.
You put down **Trade 1** reminder token next to Mathew and Marcus on the Grimoire and confirm that this Trade is happening.
At the end of the day Marcus is executed.
The following night remove the **Trade 1** reminder token from Marcus and Mathew.
***DO NOT*** move any potions from either player to the other.

Jonas asks Lisa if she wants to trade.
She declines the Trade.
***DO NOT*** put down any trade reminder tokens.
