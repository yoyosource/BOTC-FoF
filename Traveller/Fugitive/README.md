# Fugitive
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night* 2 alive players choose either 1) The other receives a Good potion, or 2) to learn the other and their potions. If both choose 2, each receive an Evil potion instead.

*I've run so far away,\
I ran all night and day,\
so far away,\
but I couldn't get away.*

The Fugitive is a source of Good potions for the town, with a potential incentive to spread Evil potions around instead,

## How to run
Each night except the first, wake up any alive player.
Tell them that the Fugitive lets them choose either 1 or 2.
Put them back to sleep.
Wake up a different alive player.
Tell them that the Fugitive lets them choose either 1 or 2.

If both choose 2, put an evil Potion on both players,
otherwise put a good Potion onto the player whose partner chose 1.
If a single player chose 2, wake them and point towards the other player and show them all Potions that player holds.

## Examples
Gary is the Fugitive, so on the 2nd night, the Storyteller wakes Mandy and Ross separately.
Both choose between 1 or 2. Both Mandy & Ross chooses 1.
The Storyteller puts a good Potion on Mandy and a good Potion on Ross.

Manuel is the Fugitive, so on the 2nd night, the Storyteller wakes up Manuel and Celeste separately.
Both choose between 1 or 2. Manuel chooses 1 while Celeste chooses 2.
The storyteller puts a good Potion onto Celeste.
After that the storyteller wakes Celeste points towards Manuel and points towards all Potions Manuel is currently holding.

Raphael is the Fugitive, so on the 2nd night, the Storyteller wakes Raphael and Konrad separately.
Both choose between 1 or 2. Both Raphael & Konrad chooses 2.
The Storyteller puts and evil Potion on Raphael and an evil Potion on Konrad.
