# Snake Oil Seller
Authors: Jonas, Marcus

## Summary

> Each night you learn a good and an evil potion. Choose either 1) which potion for a player to receive, or 2) which player to receive either potion.

*In my scientific opinion, the existence of a Demon would have an impact of gigantic proportions: mythology and legend would become history and science. One could really make a quick buck with it*

The Snake Oil Seller gets to distribute potions, either choosing type or targets.

## How to run
Each night wake the Snake Oil Seller.
Point towards one good Potion and one evil Potion.
Let them choose either a player or a Potion.
Put them back to sleep.

If they choose a player decide which of the shown Potions to give that player.
If they choose a Potion decide which player receives it.

## Examples
Max the Snake Oil Seller is woken up and shown a Potion of Curiosity and a Potion of Stress.
Max chooses 1 and points at Potion of Curiosity.
The storyteller puts the Potion of Curiosity onto any living player in the game.

Stina the Snake Oil Seller is woken up and shown the Potion of Joy and the Potion of Stress.
Stina chooses 2 and points at Ron.
The Storyteller puts EITHER a Potion of Joy or a Potion of Stress on Ron.
